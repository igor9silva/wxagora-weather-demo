# Third-party materials

## Synthetic sample presenter

The optional `assets/presenter-sample.png` is an AI-generated, fictional portrait from row 4 of [lambdaWalker/ds.photo_id](https://huggingface.co/datasets/lambdaWalker/ds.photo_id), by lambdaWalker. The dataset is offered under the [Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International licence](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). In this project the image is cropped/composited and the mouth is animated for the video. It can be replaced with an appropriately authorised portrait.

## Local models

- [MuseTalk 1.5](https://github.com/TMElyralab/MuseTalk) and [MuseTalk 1.5 MLX q8](https://huggingface.co/mlx-community/MuseTalk-1.5-q8) provide lip-sync inference. The MLX model card and port describe an MIT licence; their dependencies retain their own licences.
- [tts_eu_pt](https://github.com/logus2k/tts_eu_pt) and its [European Portuguese Kokoro weights](https://huggingface.co/logus2k/kokoro_tts_eu_pt) provide the local synthetic voice. They are distributed under Apache 2.0; see the upstream attribution notices.

## Weather data

[IPMA](https://api.ipma.pt/) is the source of the forecast snapshots. Its terms and attribution requirements apply independently of the code and other assets.
