# Artwork generation

The two project assets were made with Codex's built-in image-generation tool and copied into `assets/`. They are one-time creative assets; the forecast numbers, labels and weather data are composited later by code.

## `portugal-world.png`

> Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: reusable portrait background artwork for a 15-second Portuguese weather forecast video. Create a beautiful, cinematic, premium 3D animated-film style miniature landscape inspired by Portugal, seen from a high three-quarter camera, taller than wide. A connected rocky coastline with deep blue Atlantic water, terracotta roofs, elegant Portuguese azulejo-blue and white buildings, a distinctive Porto-style arched bridge at the upper left, a Lisbon-style suspension bridge in the middle, and sunlit southern cliffs near the lower right. Include a small green volcanic Atlantic island offshore. The composition should allow slow camera pans and crops, with generous clear blue sea around the land for separate captions and a character. The land and cities should look like a lovingly crafted miniature model with fine but readable details, soft atmospheric light, exquisite materials and a warm optimistic broadcast feel. This is a stylized illustration, not an accurate navigational map. Neutral clear sky and no rain, snow, weather symbols, labels, typography, numbers, logos, flags, or watermark. No presenter. Output only the artwork.

## `cloud-explorer.png`

> Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: transparent full-body character cutout for a playful Portuguese weather forecast video. Create a single charming, highly appealing miniature 3D animated-film weather explorer mascot, a little friendly floating cloud-shaped robot with a recognizable face, glossy expressive dark eyes, small white sculpted body, tiny cobalt blue jacket, sunshine-yellow scarf fluttering, little mitten-like hands and warm yellow boots. It carries a small simple compass. Three-quarter angle, facing the viewer, dynamic welcoming standing pose, full body completely visible, centered with margin. Rich production-quality 3D animated-film rendering, soft clean studio lighting, detailed materials, cohesive blue-white-gold palette. Transparent background with actual alpha, no ground plane, no shadow extending far outside the body. No words, lettering, logo, watermark, additional characters or scenery.
